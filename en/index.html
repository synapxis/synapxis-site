SynapXis Inc.

Turning dormant data
into decisions.

SynapXis is a data analytics and management consulting firm based in Tokyo. We bring together data that companies hold in separate places, such as customer feedback, employee surveys, sales and operations, to identify what actually drives performance. We do not stop at the report: we work with you on what to do next.

Services

What we do

01 — Analytics

Data integration and analysis

We reconnect data you have been looking at in silos and pin down, in numbers, what moves your results.

02 — Advisory

Management consulting and advisory

We turn findings into actions and stay with you until the numbers move.

03 — Outreach

Speaking, writing and supervision

Talks for industry associations and seminars, columns for trade media, and editorial supervision.

Typical themes: performance gaps between sites and stores / the link between customer satisfaction and sales / drivers of employee retention / KPI design for sales teams / evaluation of promotions. Our work covers service industries including restaurants, hotels, hot-spring and bathing facilities, beauty salons, retail and professional sports.

Founder

Who leads the work

Takato Yamaguchi

Representative Director & CEO

Takato began his career in finance and corporate planning at Sojitz Corporation, then worked as a strategy consultant at Accenture. He went on to lead a business unit at MISUMI Group and served as an Executive Officer at Accordia Golf, before joining the private equity firm Karita & Company.

He was then appointed CEO of a struggling portfolio company, ROI (now Fancrew Inc.), to lead its turnaround. He later led a fund-backed management buyout and continued to grow the business as one of its owners. Working with customer feedback data from multi-site service businesses, he developed an analytical method that combines multiple data sources to identify the drivers of performance.

He founded SynapXis in August 2026.

  • Special Advisor, Fancrew Inc.
  • Advisor, Karita & Company
  • Chair, VoR Subcommittee, Digital Committee, Shokudanren (Japanese federation of restaurant industry associations)
